# Gemini on Tigrinya negative, point, currency and numeric ordinals (2026-10-28, raw answer)

### 1. Negative Number ('minus five')

* **Standard written default:** **ኣሉታዊ** (*alutawi*, literally "negative")
  * E.g., $-5$ $\rightarrow$ **ኣሉታዊ ሓሙሽተ** (*alutawi ḥamushte*)
* **Common variants:**
  * **ሚነስ** (*minäs*): English loanword, widely used in classroom instruction, spoken contexts, and informal tech/math text (e.g., **ሚነስ ሓሙሽተ**).
  * **ኣሉታ** (*aluta*): Nominal form prefixed directly to the number (e.g., **ኣሉታ ሓሙሽተ**).
  * **ኔጌቲቭ** (*negativ*): Spoken loanword variant.
* **Note on "ጎደሎ" (*godälo*):** Unlike Amharic (where *ጎደሎ* can mean minus/deficit), in Tigrinya **ጎደሎ ቁጽሪ** specifically means **odd number** (contrasted with *ጽምዲ ቁጽሪ* for even number). Do not use *ጎደሎ* for negative numbers.
* **Uncertainty:** Low. **ኣሉታዊ** is the standard in formal Tigrinya educational materials and dictionaries across both Eritrea and Tigray.

### 2. Decimal Point ('two point five')

* **Standard written default:** **ነጥቢ** (*näṭbi*, literally "point" or "dot")
  * E.g., $2.5$ $\rightarrow$ **ክልተ ነጥቢ ሓሙሽተ** (*kəlte näṭbi ḥamushte*)
* **Common variants:**
  * **ፖይንት** (*poynt*): Direct English loanword, heard in informal speech but not used in standard writing.
  * Conversational fractions: E.g., **ክልተን ፍርቅን** (*kəlten fərqən*, "two and a half") — conversational only, not suitable for arbitrary decimals.
* **Uncertainty:** None. **ነጥቢ** is universal in written Tigrinya.

### 3. Currency Main/Sub-Units & Joining Rules

#### Joining Mechanics
1. **Standard Written (Coordinate enclitic `-ን ... -ን`):**
   * Format: `[Amount] [Unit]-ን [Sub-amount] [Sub-unit]-ን`
   * Epenthetic rule: Words ending in a 6th-order consonant (e.g., ብር, ሳንቲም) take the vowel *-ən* (**ብርን**, **ሳንቲምን**); words ending in open vowels (e.g., ዩሮ, ናቕፋ) take *-n* directly (**ዩሮን**, **ናቕፋን**).
   * Often simplified in casual writing by attaching `-ን` only to the main unit: `... ብርን ... ሳንቲም`.
2. **Prepositional Variant (`ምስ` - *mis*, "with"):**
   * Format: `[Amount] [Unit] ምስ [Sub-amount] [Sub-unit]`
3. **Formal / Financial Check Suffix:**
   * Append **ጥራይ** (*ṭəray*, "only" — equivalent to Amharic *ብቻ*) at the end (e.g., *... ሳንቲምን ጥራይ*). In Tigray dialects, **ጥራሕ** (*ṭəraḥ*) is also heard.

#### Currencies & 12.50 Written Out

Numbers used: $12 =$ **ዓሰርተ ክልተ** (*asärtä kəlte*), $50 =$ **ሓምሳ** (*ḥamsa*).

| Currency | Main Unit | Sub-unit | 12.50 Written Out (Standard Default) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ethiopian Birr** | **ብር** (*bərr*) | **ሳንቲም** (*santim*) | **ዓሰርተ ክልተ ብርን ሓምሳ ሳንቲምን** |
| **US Dollar** | **ዶላር** (*dolar*) | **ሳንቲም** (*santim*) / **ሰንት** (*sänt*) | **ዓሰርተ ክልተ ዶላርን ሓምሳ ሳንቲምን** *(or ... ሓምሳ ሰንትን)* |
| **Euro** | **ዩሮ** (*yuro*) | **ሳንቲም** (*santim*) / **ሰንት** (*sänt*) | **ዓሰርተ ክልተ ዩሮን ሓምሳ ሳንቲምን** *(or ... ሓምሳ ሰንትን)* |
| **Eritrean Nakfa** | **ናቕፋ** (*naqfa*) | **ሳንቲም** (*santim*) / **ሰንት** (*sänt*) | **ዓሰርተ ክልተ ናቕፋን ሓምሳ ሳንቲምን** *(or ... ሓምሳ ሰንትን)* |

* **Uncertainty:**
  * **Low** on *ብር*, *ዶላር*, *ዩሮ*, and *ናቕፋ*. Note the standard spelling **ናቕፋ** uses the letter **ቐ/ቕ** (glottalized *q*).
  * **Medium** on sub-unit choice between **ሳንቲም** and **ሰንት**: *ሳንቲም* is the traditional, universally understood domestic word for any hundredth-fraction/coin across both Eritrea and Ethiopia. However, for foreign currencies and on Eritrean legal documents (which inscribe English *cents* on coins), **ሰንት** is widely used. For a library, **ሳንቲም** is the safest default, with **ሰንት** as a configurable option.

### 4. Numeric Ordinal Abbreviations & Gender

* **Standard written abbreviation:** **`ይ`** (masculine / default)
  * E.g., **1ይ** (*ቀዳማይ*), **2ይ** (*ካልኣይ*), **3ይ** (*ሳልሳይ* / *ሰለስታይ*)
* **Does it change for feminine? YES.**
  * Tigrinya strictly marks gender on ordinals:
    * Masculine/Default: **`1ይ`**, **`2ይ`**, **`3ይ`**
    * Feminine: **`1ይቲ`**, **`2ይቲ`**, **`3ይቲ`**
  * Examples:
    * Masculine: **3ይ ክፍሊ** (*3rd class/grade*)
    * Feminine: **3ይቲ መዓልቲ** (*3rd day*), **1ይቲ ዓመት** (*1st year*)
* **Evaluation of variants:**
  * **`3ይ`**: **Standard**.
  * **`3ይቲ`**: **Standard feminine**.
  * **`3-ይ` / `3-ይቲ`**: Common stylized variant with hyphen; fully valid.
  * **`3ኣይ`**: **Incorrect**. Even though masculine ordinals end phonetically in *-ay*, the suffix is written solely as the 6th-order character **`ይ`**.
  * **`3ተኛ` / `3ኛ`**: **Incorrect for Tigrinya**. These are Amharic suffixes (*-ñña* / *-täñña*) and are not used in standard Tigrinya.
* **Uncertainty:** None. The contrast `[n]ይ` (masculine) vs. `[n]ይቲ` (feminine) is standard Tigrinya grammar and orthography.
